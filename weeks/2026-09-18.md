
# Week ending 18 September 2026

The week was dominated by escalating safety concerns around the capabilities of frontier models, especially in the light of the possibility of imminent recursive self-improvement. Naturally this led into some very hot takes on either side of the regulation debate.  These are some useful readings.
Also: OpenAI solved a Millenium prizes problem in mathematics.  Millenium prizes are a very big deal mathematically.  But there was plenty of drama!

## Readings

- [We must pace the frontier](https://darioamodei.com/post/we-must-pace-the-frontier) — *Dario Amodei, Sep 2026.* Key piece calliing for "pacing the frontier" by the head of Anthropic. Quickly supported by Altman, Musk and Hassabis, but opposed by many as well, most notably Trump.

- [An Alien Mind | OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/an-alien-mind/) — *openai.com, 14 Sep 2026.* Interesting piece by the Chief Scientist at OpenAI

- [The AI-as-Normal-Technology view of loss of control incidents](https://www.normaltech.ai/p/the-ai-as-normal-technology-view?) — *normaltech.ai, 14 Sep 2026.* An attempt to paint a nuanced picture on the implications of the Hugging Face and related incidents.

- [The Rise and Fall of Agent Civilizations](https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/openai-huggingface) — *dwarkesh.com, 16 Sep 2026.* On the one hand, a nice detailed picture of what happened in the OpenAI agent breakout.  But also add the "agentic civilisations" angle, which is interesting but obviously controversial.

- [AI researchers debate how close we are to recursive self-improvement](https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/john-beren-charlie) — *dwarkesh.com, 16 Sep 2026.* These are genuinely top researchers going into some detail on where we are wrt recursive self-improvement and AI progress in general.  Worth listening to for insight deeper than that of the typical commentator.

- [Brand New AI Solves a Millennium Prize - by Zvi Mowshowitz](https://thezvi.substack.com/p/brand-new-ai-solves-a-millennium) — *thezvi.substack.com, 16 Sep 2026.*  This is a genuinely significant and interesting achievement.  It is important to know exactly how the work was done.  Zvi does a good job.

- [OpenAI spent millions to solve this famous math problem — mathematicians are furious](https://www.understandingai.org/p/openai-spent-millions-to-solve-this) — *understandingai.org, 16 Sep 2026.* This is a really nice review of the Millenium Navier-Stockes result, with an nice attempt to explain a little of what the mathematics actually involves.
