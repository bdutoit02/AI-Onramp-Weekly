# Week ending 2 October 2026

A range of topics and formats this week.  A significant event at the White House, which we filter through Zvi's inimitable take.  Lots of colour (including the table arrangement!). Then Ezra Klein interviews two big fish from either side of the regulatory/accelerationist debate. Plus an interview with Garrison Lovely, a historically rare left-liberal voice getting to grips with the technical complexities.  Lastly some informed takes on some key issues of the moment: Noam Brown from OpenAI, on Dwarkesh, discussing some current frontier issues on agents, alignment and recursive self-improvement, a note from Nathan Lambert with a little more on what RSI actually promises, and lastly an interesting note from Ethan Mollick on the dots, swarms and the latest version of the Bitter Lesson.
